⚠ Demonstration & Data Safety Notice

SCCA 2.3 is a demonstration and portfolio project only. It does not collect, store, process or transmit patient data and does not connect to SystmOne, NHS Spine, HSCN or any other live clinical system.

All terminology examples and correspondence examples are fictional/demo content. The application must not be used for real patient care, clinical decision-making or production coding.

A production implementation would require appropriate clinical safety, information governance, cybersecurity, terminology release management, testing and local organisational approval.
